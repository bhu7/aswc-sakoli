About the institution

Knowledge that opens doors.

Managed by Shyam Bahuuddeshiya Shikshan Sanstha, Sakoli, Aai Shakuntala Mahila Mahavidyalaya is recognised by the Government of Maharashtra and affiliated to Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, Nagpur.

Our undergraduate programmes in Science and Commerce support academic development and encourage students to participate in sports and cultural activities.
